# Predictive Sales & Demand Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing sales and demand data using Microsoft Excel and Power BI. The dashboard provides insights into sales performance, profitability, product demand, seasonal patterns, delivery performance, and future sales trends.

## Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales, profit, and quantity performance
- Identify high-performing and low-performing products
- Understand monthly and yearly sales trends
- Identify peak sales months and seasonal patterns
- Analyze product demand and profitability
- Evaluate delivery performance and delivery delays
- Analyze supplier performance
- Identify fast-moving and slow-moving products
- Support better business decision-making
